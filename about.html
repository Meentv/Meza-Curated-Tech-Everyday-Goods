About Meza

Curated tech & everyday goods.

Fewer, better things

Meza started with a simple idea: most catalogs are noise. We keep a short list of technology and everyday essentials and give each one room to be understood — real specifications, honest availability and a clear price.

Every product page tells you what the item is, what it does and what it is made of. There are no countdown timers, no invented review scores and no inflated "best seller" badges — just the details you'd want before deciding.

A demo storefront, honestly labeled

Meza is a fictional store built as a frontend portfolio project. Products, brands and prices are invented; the cart and wishlist live in your browser's localStorage; and checkout is a simulation — no payment is collected and no order is ever transmitted.

This site is built with vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript ES modules — no frameworks, no build step — so the code stays readable and maintainable.
